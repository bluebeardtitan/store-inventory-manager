How it's wired

This page is a static site with no server of its own. It talks directly to the Google Sheets and Drive APIs using the Google account you sign in with. Your store's data lives in a spreadsheet in that account's Drive, and its settings live in a small config file under Drive › MAK-Projects › Store-Manager. Nothing passes through any third-party server.
